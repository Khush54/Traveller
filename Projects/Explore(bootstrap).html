Andhra Pradesh

Araku Valley

Araku Valley, known for its coffee plantations and tribal culture, is a serene landscape offering breathtaking views. Ideal for those looking to immerse themselves in nature’s beauty and tranquility.

Srikalahasti: The Temple

Srikalahasti is revered for its magnificent Shiva temple, one of the five elemental lingas of Shiva. This town is not just a spiritual haven but also a center for the ancient art of Kalamkari a type of hand-painted textile.

Vijayawada: The Land of Victory

Vijayawada, cradled on the banks of the Krishna River, is famed for its historical prowess and natural splendor. Vijayawada’s undulating hills and lush landscapes invite adventurers and nature lovers alike.

Visakhapatnam: The Jewel

Visakhapatnam is the city’s scenic landscapes, from the Beach Road to the Eastern Ghats, offer a plethora of activities for tourists. Submarine Museum,unique attraction that showcase India’s naval prowess.

Anantapur: The Heritage Heaven

Anantapur beckons with its rich tapestry of history and culture. The city is a treasure trove of architectural wonders and ancient sites, including the magnificent Lepakshi Temple and the serene Penukonda Fort.

Rushikonda Beach

Rushikonda Beach is renowned for its golden sands and vibrant marine life, making it a heaven for water sports enthusiasts. The beach is surrounded by lush greenery, adding to its beauty and tranquility.

Bihar

Glass Floor Bridge

One of the most exciting architectural marvels coming up in Bihar is the Glass-Floor Bridge, a massive skywalk whose specialty is its transparent floor. The bridge is being constructed in the midst of five picturesque hills in the Rajgir area of Bihar.

Thai Monastery

The magnificent Thai Monastery ,established back in 1957 ,aims at preaching the philosophies of Buddhism. The architecture of the monastery and the customs and rituals practiced here offer a deep insight of the rich Thai culture.

Bihar Museum

The Bihar Museum is a popular attraction in Patna that gives visitors an insight into Bihar's past while instilling pride and reverence in the hearts of Young visitors and it has been recent addition to the list of attractions.

Nalanda University

Nalanda University is amongst the earliest pioneers of learning. In addition to being a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the university has origins that date back to as early as the 3rd century, and the entire structure spreads across 14 hectares of land.

Tutla Bhawani Waterfall

Tutla Bhawani waterfall is surrounded by two gigantic mountains, which converge to form a spectacular vedant valley. There is also a charming waterfall in the middle with the serene Kachuar river flowing amidst the valley.

Takht Sri Patna Sahib

The Takht Sri Patna Sahib is a gorgeous, sprawling gurudwara, built on the banks of the Ganges in honour of Shri Guru Gobind Singh. The gurdwara was built in the 18th century by Maharaja Ranjit Singh as a symbol of respect and devotion to his guru.

Jammu & Kashmir

Kargil

The beautiful city of Kargil is the second largest in Ladakh region in Jammu and Kashmir after Leh. Known for its rich cultural, historical and political importance, this city sits at 2676 meters above sea level, along the banks of the Suru or Indus River.

Jammu

The visit to Jammu is all about the pleasure of five senses, and it opens a window of the Himalayan lifestyle of the discerning traveler. Visitors can choose to go on a nature trail to encounter rare avian species or take part in adventure activities.

Gulmarg

Gulmarg to explore the backing stands of the splendid mature pines traversing you to the elevation of bald Mt Afarwat. To make it to the mountain, you can either trek on foot or take help from ponies at a meager amount of INR 300 per hour.

Patnitop

Patnitop is a must-visit place in Jammu & Kashmir. It offers beautiful views of the Chenab Basin and Pir Panjal Valley. With charming meadows and natural freshwater springs, it is a hub for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Pir Panjal Mountain Range

The Pir Panjal Mountain Range is a majestic and rugged range in the Indian state of Jammu and Kashmir. It is renowned for its stunning landscapes, including lush valleys, towering peaks, and picturesque alpine meadows.

Kashmir

Kashmir where the majestic Chinar trees, the enchanting sounds of the Santoor, and snow-capped Himalayas come together to create unparalleled beauty. Infinite memories in a snug Kahwa teacup while enjoying its picture-perfect landscapes.

Delhi

Jantar Mantar

Jantar Mantar constructed by Maharaja Jai Singh II of Jaipur, the one in which Delhi has an equal reputation as of the Jaipur one. At Delhi Jantar Mantar, there are 13 architectural astronomy instruments.

Jama Masjid

The largest mosques in India which was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan between 1644 and 1656. The original name of the mosque meant the mosque that commanding view of the world.

ISKCON Temple

The Iskcon Temple is a spectacular shrine located in Delhi constituted to be the best tourist places in Delhi. Situated at the Sant Nagar in East Kailash, Iskcon Temple dwarfs the southern skyline zone of Delhi.

National Museum

A visit to the National Museum of India will be a lifetime experience. This largest repository in India contains many prominent artworks dated over 5000 years ago and it is one of the best places in east Delhi.

Rashtrapathi Bhavan

The Presidential Residence, formerly known as the Viceroy’s House is the famous Rashtrapathi Bhavan in Delhi. It is the home of the President of India, officially. It is located at the western end of Rajpath.

Lotus Temple

The Lotus Temple is among the famous tourist places in Delhi and a major landmark of India that has been attracting tourists from all over the world because of its architecture and beauty.

Karnataka

Shravanabelagola

Shravanabelagola a major holiday attraction in Karnataka is famous for the statue of Lord Gomateswara also known as Bahubali statue. It is believed to be the tallest monolithic stone statue.

Pattadakal

Pattadakal is a World Heritage site for its historical monuments and temples.Pattadakal used to be under the Badami Chalukyas and previously it was called Raktapura.

Yana

If you love unique places, natural surroundings and fantastic landscapes then Yana is one of the best places in Karnataka. Yana is a small village and hill station located just 140 km away from the city Hubli.

Goa

Mandovi River Cruise

Calming experiences over adventure sports and clubbing, Mandovi River Cruise is among the best activities you can try in Goa. Just board one of the luxury cruises and set out on a journey to embrace peace and enjoy on-board entertainment.

Grand Island

Grand Island is the nirvana of Goan water-sports. There is a lot to be experienced here from diving to snorkelling to fishing. Scuba diving in Goa is the most popular activity because it offers so much in terms of underwater exploration.

Dudhsagar Waterfalls

The Dudhsagar Waterfalls are situated on the Goa-Karnataka border and are one of India’s must-see sights. The four-tiered waterfall structure is one of the tallest in India and is located on the Mandovi River.You can enjoy photographic scene.

Maharashtra

Harihareshwar Beach

Harihareshwar draws in the visitors with its scenic backdrops, vibrant streets, and hilly landscapes. The pristine river Saviriti passing through this scenic town further accentuates its charm.

Harishchandragad

Settled at 4665 ft, Harishchandragad is an ancient hill fort of Ahmednagar district. This hill fort holds relevance to the history of Malshej Ghat and Kothale village in protecting and controlling the entire region.

Mumbai

Mumbai, the old world charm and fast pace of the modern life. The USP of places to visit in Mumbai is that they are all iconic and all you need is a picture to recognize them, that is the charm of this city.

Punjab

Golden Temple

With its magnificent beauty, the iconic history of the Golden Temple dates back to 400 years. It is the most significant shrine in Sikhism.

Chandigarh

Chandigarh shows itself off to global travellers much like greater India would wish to be seen – prosperous, comfortable and cosmopolitan.

Anandpur Sahib

Anandpur Sahib is located in the Roper district of Punjab founded by the ninth Sikh Guru- Guru Tegh Bahadur in 1665.

Rajasthan

Albert Museum

Albert Hall Museum in Jaipur is one of the best and captivating Tourist Places in Rajasthan. Also known as the Central Museum is situated amidst the lawn of Ram Niwas Bagh in Jaipur.

Lake Pichola

Lake Pichola is one of the most popular tourist places in Rajasthan. It is an artificial lake which has been named after the Picholi village and was created in 1362 AD.

Amber Fort

The Amber Fort, more generally known as the Amer Fort, is one of the most popular and is situated at a distance of 11 km from Jaipur, the capital city of Rajasthan.

Tamil Nadu

Kolli Hills

The majestic Kolli Hills is a trivial mountain range positioned in Western Tamil Nadu, in the Namakkal district. Popular as Kolli malai among the local people, the hills rise to an altitude of over 4,265 feet.

Bear Shola Falls

Bear Shola Falls, nestled in the heart of the quaint hill station of Kodaikanal in Tamil Nadu, India, is a scenic cascade that captivates visitors with its natural charm.Its natural presence make it more attractive.

Ooty

From nature walks in gardens that makes one wonder whether Paradise, as depicted in scriptures of various cultures, have actually been derived from such sensory experiences.

Haryana

Kurukshetra Panorama & Science Centre

The Kurukshetra Panorama & Science Centre was founded to display the influence of science on the religious beliefs in India. It is utilized for showcasing interactive science exhibits that highlight of technology with traditional Indian culture.

Mojoland

Mojoland is an incredible amusement, adventure and water park located on the highway on GT Karnal Road, near Murthal. It is situated strategically in Sonepat, the outskirts of Delhi and is bound to catch your eye if you are travelling along the road.

Brahma Sarovar

This sacred reservoir has been mentioned in the writings of Al Beruni. It is among the principal places to see in Kurukshetra. Thousands throng the bathing fair here on a solar eclipse, with the belief that taking a dip would provide eternal salvation.

Uttarakhand

Auli

Located amidst the majestic Garhwal Himalayas, Auli rewards tourists with stunning snow-capped mountains, vistas and coniferous forests. It is also a much-preferred destination for sports lovers because tourists can enjoy snowboarding and skiing.

Mussoorie

Located amidst the magnificent Garhwal Himalayas, this hill station is one of the most beautiful tourist places in Uttarakhand. Leisure travellers and honeymooners have chosen Mussoorie as their preferred destination.

Haridwar

This place is famously known as the 'Gateway to the Gods', and it is an important pilgrimage destination for Hindus. Situated along the banks of river Ganga, Haridwar is famous for its vibrant culture and numerous temples.
