HISTORICAL PLACES

TAJ MAHAL
Eternal Symbol Of Love

Built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan, carved with white marble mausoleum grace the southern bank of the Yamuna River and is one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

AGRA FORT
The Historical Charm

Agra Fort was a walled city that was built during the reign of Emperor Akbar. It boasts intricate artwork on marble and sandstone walls and is a medley of different architectural styles.

QUTUB MINAR
Beauty Etched In Stone

Qutub Minar is one of the tallest monuments. Featuring beautiful carvings and intricate designs on marble and red sandstone, this architectural wonder is one of photographer’s delight.

INDIA GATE
Honor Stand Eternal

This iconic sandstone-granite arch gate is among the largest war memorials in the country. The beauty of this important monument is accentuated by the verdant lawns that surround it.

HAWA MAHAL
Charm Of The Palace

This is a wonderful blend of Rajput, Islamic and Mughal architectural styles, which makes it totally Insta-worthy. Hawa Mahal offers stunning views of the City Palace and Jantar Mantar from the top, too.

GATEWAY OF INDIA
Standing Tall & Proud

This unique landmark is one of the most important monuments of India. The majestic gateway, you can enjoy the cool sea breeze, relish some delectable street foods, or go for a leisurely ferry ride.
